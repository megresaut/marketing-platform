Concierge-fit signals are strong

💬 Suggested angles

Drawn from the angles that closed similar Concierge prospects.

Lead with time, not service
For owners like James, the pain isn't "I need a property manager" — it's "I can't think about my house when I'm trying to do my job." Frame Concierge around reclaiming his calendar, not around the service catalog. Three of your last four Concierge closes opened with this framing.
Reference the Thornton precedent
The Thorntons (Old Greenwich primary + Aspen) closed three weeks ago at $1,600/mo. Similar profile, similar two-home setup. A neighbor reference like this typically lands well — he likely knows of them or their type.
Vendor-pass-through differentiator
AVM doesn't markup vendor invoices. Worth mentioning once, naturally — most competitors do markup 10–20%, and the saved margin alone usually pays for the management fee on a multi-property owner. Don't lead with price, but be ready if he asks.

He may have shopped around
Profile suggests methodical decision-maker. There's a non-trivial chance he's already gotten quotes from competitors. If a number gets thrown out, don't react — anchor to value (vendor pass-through, single point of accountability, response time) instead of competing on price.
Decision-maker check
No signal about a spouse or co-owner in profile data. Worth a soft check early on — "is this typically a decision you make on your own or is there someone else involved" — so a second meeting can be scheduled if needed.
